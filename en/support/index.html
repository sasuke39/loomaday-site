Need help with Looma, billing, or your account? Email support@loomaday.com (typical response time: 1–2 business days).

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my Pro subscription?
    Open Looma → Me → Looma Pro → tap Restore Purchases.
  • How do I cancel auto-renewal?
    Go to your Apple device's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions and select Looma.
  • How do I permanently delete my account?
    Open Looma → Me → Account & Subscription → Delete Account.
  • Can I use my own AI model (Ollama / OpenAI / DeepSeek)?
    Yes — open Me → Connections → AI Assistant and enter your endpoint and model.
