Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · App: Looma

Looma is built as a privacy-respecting, local-first personal companion. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use Looma.

1. Local-First Storage

Your calendar events, to-dos, reminders, habits, knowledge base notes, journals, and long-term memories are stored locally on your device in a sandboxed SQLite database. All core local features work without creating an account.

2. Third-Party & Cloud AI Processing

The AI assistant is optional. Messages and explicitly referenced context are only sent to an external AI provider (or Looma managed cloud models) after you review and grant explicit in-app consent. You may also connect a local model (such as Ollama on localhost) so no data leaves your device. We never sell your personal data or use it for advertising or third-party model training.

3. On-Device Vision & Speech

Image text extraction (Apple Vision) and voice dictation (Apple Speech) run locally on your Apple device. Extracted or dictated text is only shared with your chosen AI provider when you explicitly submit a message.

4. Account, Subscriptions & Deletion

If you sign in (Sign in with Apple or email verification) or subscribe to Looma Pro, we store only the minimum account identifiers, email address, and subscription status required to provide cloud sync and subscription access.

You may permanently delete your account and all cloud data anytime in-app via Me → Account & Subscription → Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email us at support@loomaday.com.
